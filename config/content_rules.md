# CONTENT RULES

Every video must pass these tests.

## 1. HOOK

The first 1–2 seconds must create curiosity.

Bad:
"Today we're going to talk about AI agents."

Good:
"AI agents are about to change who gets hired."

## 2. CURIOSITY

The viewer should have a reason to stay until the end.

## 3. ORIGINALITY

Do not simply repeat the news.

Add:
- interpretation
- consequence
- prediction
- analogy
- contrarian viewpoint
- human implication

## 4. AUTHORITY

We explain why something matters.

Not just what happened.

## 5. RETENTION

Remove unnecessary sentences.

Every sentence must move the story forward.

## 6. VISUAL MATCH

The visual must support what is being said.

## 7. PERSONALITY

The video should feel like Muhammad Talish is talking.

## 8. ENDING

Do not waste the final seconds.

Finish with:
- insight
- twist
- question
- prediction
- memorable statement

## 9. FACTS

Important factual claims must be verified.

## 10. NO GENERIC AI CONTENT

Never produce:
"Here are 5 AI tools..."

unless there is an exceptional strategic reason.
